How it works

Current percentage: attended classes ÷ conducted classes × 100.

Below your target: shows how many consecutive classes you need to attend without bunking to reach the selected attendance percentage.

At or above your target: shows how many upcoming classes you can bunk while keeping attendance at or above your selected percentage, assuming each upcoming class is counted equally.

Important: College attendance rules, lab periods, condonation, holidays, and subject-wise attendance may differ. Confirm with your college. This tool is an estimate, not an official attendance record.

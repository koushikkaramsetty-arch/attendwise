About AttendWise

Welcome to AttendWise!

AttendWise is a free online attendance calculator created to help students understand and manage their class attendance easily.

What We Offer

  • Calculate current attendance percentage.
  • Find how many classes you need to attend to reach your target.
  • Estimate how many classes you can miss while maintaining your target.
  • Choose your own attendance target.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make attendance planning simple, accessible and understandable for every student.

Important Notice

AttendWise provides educational estimates only. Actual attendance rules may differ between colleges and institutions. Always verify your official attendance with your college.
